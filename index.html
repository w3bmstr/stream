Android Camera → WebRTC Stream

Step 1 (Android phone): Choose “Camera Sender”, start camera, then click “Create Offer”. Copy the offer text and send it to your PC.

Step 2 (PC): Choose “Viewer”, paste the offer, click “Create Answer”, then copy the answer back to the phone.

Step 3 (Android phone): Paste the answer and click “Set Remote Answer”. The video should appear on the PC.
